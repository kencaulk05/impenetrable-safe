Control Space

Settings

The starting values are the ones that worked on your door. Calibrate reads the closed distance from the sensor (the door must be closed and still for 3 seconds); do it again after the motor closes the door, because the closed distance may differ a little from when you closed it by hand. The settings are kept in a file on the board.

What to try, in order

  1. Calibrate. With the door closed and still, press "Calibrate the closed distance". It should say about 96 mm.
  2. Open and close. Press the Space bar: the door opens, and a moment later the log says "The door is open" (the sensor checked it). Press Space again: it closes and the log says "The door is closed". If a move did not work you get a retry, then a FAULT.
  3. Tamper. With the door closed (LOCKED), push it open by hand by about 1 cm. Within a third of a second the page says TAMPER. Let go, and when the door is closed again the alarm ends.
  4. Blocked. Hold your hand a few centimetres in front of the sensor: the door reads "blocked" and that is TAMPER too.
  5. Push back. Tick "Push back when forced" and push the door open a little: the motor tries once to push it shut. (If you hold it open, it gives up after one retry and shows a FAULT.)
