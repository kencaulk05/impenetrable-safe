What to try, in order

  1. Aim. Watch the big number while you point the sensor at the inside of the door. It should be steady and make sense (about how far is it, in millimetres?). No number, or "no echo": check the wiring and the power.
  2. Power test. With the sensor on 3V3, capture "door closed" (say 5 s). Then move its VCC wire to 5V, choose 5V above, and capture again. Compare the "usable readings" and the "spread". If 3V3 gives at least 90% usable and a small spread, keep 3V3.
  3. Door closed. Capture "door closed": this is the distance X the lock will watch for.
  4. Tamper. With the door closed, push it open by hand about 1 cm and capture; then about 3 cm and capture. The distance should grow, and the sensor should still see the door.
  5. Door fully open. Open the door all the way and capture. If the door has left the sensor's view, no echo, or a far distance, is the right answer (it is not a fault): the lock will treat that as "door open".

How to read the verdict (for "door closed", "pushed open" and "other"): GOOD = spread of 2 mm or less; OK = up to 5 mm; POOR = jumpy, or fewer than 90% usable readings; BAD = fewer than half usable (nothing useful is coming back). For "door fully open" the verdict says whether nothing is in view (expected), or something far away is, or the wall behind is.
