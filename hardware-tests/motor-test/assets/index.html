Safety. Hold the door the first time and keep fingers out of its swing. STOP: press Esc, or the Space bar while the motor is moving. The board ends every move by itself after at most 3 seconds, even if this page goes away. Do not hold the door shut against the motor.

Settings

Start gently: a speed of about 100 and 300 ms. Raise the time until the door reaches the end, then rate each move below. If the door goes the wrong way for "open", tick Swap direction. The soft start climbs from the lowest speed to the chosen speed over that time, which spares the 5 V rail when the motor shares it with the board. The settings are remembered after a restart.

What to try, in order

  1. Direction. With the door held, press the Space bar once at a low speed (about 100) and a short time (300 ms). Which way did the door go? If it went the wrong way for "open", tick Swap direction.
  2. Open and close fully. Raise the time (and the speed a little if the door barely moves) until it reaches the end without grinding. After each move, say how it went.
  3. Find the shortest time that works, for opening and for closing separately (they can differ), at the lowest speed that moves the door reliably.
  4. Watch for trouble: the board resetting or the webcam dropping out when the motor starts (the motor shares the power), a hum without movement, or grinding at the end.
