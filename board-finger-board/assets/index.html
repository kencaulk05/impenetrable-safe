Arrow keys move the square by 4 pixels (hold Shift for 16); the + and − keys make it bigger or smaller. The network was trained on the square at x 108, y 163, side 317 (at 640 × 480): the closer you get to it, the better it works. The saved square is used the next time the app starts.

What to try, in order

  1. Is the camera found? The "camera" chip at the top should turn green and name the webcam. If it says "no camera found yet", plug the webcam into the hub (the app looks again every 5 seconds).
  2. Put the square on the tape. Press "Find the tape square", check the green square sits just inside your tape, adjust with the arrow keys and + and −, then press Save. The camera must be fixed in place, like when the training pictures were taken.
  3. Show your hand in the square with 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 fingers (thumb folded for 1 to 4, open hand for 5, a fist or an empty square for 0). The answer and the bars should follow, and "steady answer" should turn green after about a second.
  4. Benchmark. Press the button and write down "one step" and "steps / s". Then run compare/bench_numpy.py on your laptop for the same numbers: that is the UNO Q against the computer.
